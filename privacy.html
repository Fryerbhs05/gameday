Privacy Policy

Conflicted is a fantasy-football companion that pulls your leagues together from ESPN, Yahoo, and Sleeper into one view, so you can see where your rosters help you and where they fight each other. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to get it deleted.

This Privacy Policy is part of, and incorporated into, our Terms of Use.

The short version. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and we do not share your fantasy data with anyone. You can use Conflicted without an account. If you make one, you can delete it — and everything attached to it — from inside the app at any time.

11. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit — all traffic is served over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest for credentials — any stored platform session is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it reaches our database, so the database only ever holds unreadable ciphertext.
  • Passwordless sign-in — we use one-time magic links. We store only a hashed, short-lived version of the token; it works once and expires in 15 minutes. There is no password to steal.
  • Least privilege — row-level security is enabled on our database with no public access policies; all reads and writes go through our server functions.
  • Rate limiting — abusive request volumes are throttled at the edge.
  • Bounded analytics — event names and properties are validated and size-capped so credentials cannot leak into analytics.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping access to your email account and device secure.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights. We extend the core rights below to all users regardless of location:

  • Know / access — what personal information we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Delete — request erasure of your personal information.
  • Correct — fix inaccurate personal information.
  • Portability — receive a copy in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Opt out — of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling with legal effects (we do none of these).
  • Withdraw consent — for marketing or optional profile fields, at any time.
  • Non-discrimination — we will never degrade the Service or charge you differently for exercising a privacy right.
  • Appeal — if we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision; we will respond within 45 days. If we deny the appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General.

EEA/UK users additionally have the right to restrict or object to processing and to lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

14. How to exercise your rights

In the app (fastest):

  • Disconnect a platform — removes that platform's stored session.
  • Delete your account — permanently erases your email, name, profile fields, league connections, and every stored session. This is immediate and irreversible.

By email: Send your request to ConflictedApp@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We use that to verify identity; for sensitive requests we may ask you to confirm via a one-time link. We will acknowledge within 10 business days and respond substantively within 45 calendar days, extendable once by another 45 days with notice.

Authorized agents: An agent may submit a request on your behalf with written, signed permission. We may ask you to verify your identity directly.
